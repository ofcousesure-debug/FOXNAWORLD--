**Purpose & context**

氣菇 engages Claude for creative writing projects, particularly short fiction derived from visual source material (e.g., manga-style images). The work involves translating visual storytelling into written narrative with attention to character identity, perspective, and dramatic structure.

**Current state**

A recent session involved writing short fiction (~500 characters per section) based on manga-style images featuring a father-daughter pair with secret dual identities—the daughter as the horned villain, the father as the magical girl, neither aware of the other's secret. Multiple versions were produced: dual limited-perspective drafts and a unified third-person narrative. The session required correcting an initial misreading of character roles before producing accurate drafts.

**Key learnings & principles**

- 氣菇 provides precise directorial corrections when character identity or POV assignments are wrong—these should be treated as authoritative and acted on immediately.
- Dramatic irony (characters ignorant of each other's secret identity) is a core creative constraint to preserve across versions.
- Unsolicited additions (e.g., plot summaries appended after drafts) are likely unnecessary—stick to what's requested unless asked otherwise.

**Approach & patterns**

- Iterative refinement is the norm: 氣菇 typically requests multiple versions with specific structural variations (per-character POV, then unified third-person) rather than settling on a first draft.
- Character count targets are treated as meaningful constraints, not rough guidelines.
- Corrections are precise and directorial in nature—氣菇 specifies exactly what changed and what the corrected version should look like.
